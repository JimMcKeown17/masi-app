# Printout pack brief — Reading Card 2026.3, WelaPLUS 2026.1 and Full EGRA

**Issued: 2026-10-26.** This is the production brief for Masi's designer. It supersedes the
WelaPLUS 2024.1 brief issued 2026-10-23 (Grid A 6 × 10, Side B 5 × 8, 2024.1 footers), which
stays in git history. The 2024.1 cards are never printed: EAs receive these cards and the new
app at the same time, and the cards are printed **after the app is finished**, so paper and
phone match exactly ([spec §7](../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-egra-content-and-skip-rules-design.md#7-rollout-printing-and-reporting)).
No app access is needed to lay out this pack. Engineering does not generate the print PDFs.

One **Reading Card per language** serves WelaPLUS, Full EGRA and the two quick tests. Its letters,
words and story come unchanged from the DBE EGRA charts (isiZulu: the SMRS EGRA), so every
programme and every test reads the same content.

| Language | Reading Card sides | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| English (EN) | A, B, C, D | WelaPLUS (A–D), Full EGRA (A–C), quick tests (A, B) |
| isiXhosa (XH) | A, B, C, D | WelaPLUS (A–D), Full EGRA (A–C), quick tests (A, B) |
| Afrikaans (AF) | A, B, C | Full EGRA, quick tests |
| isiZulu (ZU) | A, B, C | Full EGRA, quick tests |

WelaPLUS 2026.1 runs in English and isiXhosa only, so only those two cards have side D, and
only those two languages get Picture Cards and Writing Sheets.

Story comprehension (WelaPLUS Q9, Full EGRA Q4) has no printed material: the EA reads the
questions from the phone. Provide editable masters and print-ready PDFs per language. For the
Reading Card, choose two A4 cards (A/B and C/D, or C/blank for AF and ZU) or one A3 folded
format. Keep every side easy to identify, with a readable footer on each content side and room
clear of trimming/folding. Paper weight, bleed, finishing and final type size are designer proof
decisions, not changes to the assessment content.

## Typography, content boundaries and footers

Use **Andika Regular** for child-facing letters, words, sentences and stories. Preserve case,
punctuation, order, repeated items, printed rows and paragraph breaks exactly as below, including
what looks like a typo in the source charts (they are reproduced as DBE printed them). Do not
replace letters with pictures, alter spelling, de-duplicate, alphabetise, or shorten a story to
fit. Keep the child's material free of answers, scoring aids and EA scripts; the phone supplies
the scripts, timers and marking controls.

**The printed rows are load-bearing.** The app stops a letters or words test, and WelaPLUS skips
the later reading and writing Questions, when the child gets **every item in the first row**
wrong. The first printed row must therefore be exactly the first row below: 10 letters on
side A; 8 words on side B (5 for isiZulu). Do not reflow rows to fit the page. The phone may
lay items out differently within those rows; the paper is not a facsimile of the phone.

**Practice items** (shown under each side below) are read before the timed test and are never
scored. Print them apart from the grid, clearly labelled as practice, as the DBE learner chart
does on the source page cited for each side. Some practice prompts also appear inside the
scored grid (for example English letters `d`, `h` and `v`); keep both.

Every printed footer must be one of these exact strings. The middle dot, spaces, version,
language code and lower-case `side` are intentional. Print the text itself, without a
`Footer:` prefix, a Question number or an extra item name.

## Reading Card content, sides A–C

Read every letter table left to right, top to bottom. Column numbers are reference aids for this
brief only: print the letters without row or column numbers. Word blocks are one printed row per
line. Stories keep the paragraphs shown, with natural line wrapping at the approved print size;
do not number words or add a scoring grid. A proof must not gain or lose an item through line
breaks, hyphenation or a substituted text.

## Writing Sheet — WelaPLUS Q5, Q10, Q11 and Q12 (EN and XH)

WelaPLUS 2026.1 numbers its writing Questions 10–12 because Story comprehension is now Q9. The
sheet's content is unchanged from the 2024 masters apart from these numbers and the footer.

**Header:** child's name, school, grade, date, EA name and a clearly boxed **Assessment code**
field with the guide `___-___`. Leave space to handwrite all six characters and the hyphen. The
EA copies the code from the phone. Do not preprint an example code.

**Q5 letter writing:** 26 picture cells, each with an empty writing box, in the 2024 grid's
six-column order (four full rows, then two cells). The **first printed row** (`a f k o s w`) is
the row WelaPLUS checks: if all six are wrong, the reading and writing Questions are skipped.
The mapping below is **designer reference only**: print pictures and blank boxes, never the
expected letters, picture names or glosses.

| Row | Expected letters (reference only) | EN picture sequence | XH picture sequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a f k o s w | apple; fish; key; orange; sun; whale | apple (i-apile); tortoise (ufudo); cat (ikati); orange (i-orenji); bread (isonka); watch (iwatchi) |
| 2 | b g l p t x | bed; gate; leaf; pot; table; x-ray | milk (ubisi); drum; sun (ilanga); pear (i-pere); table (itafile); clock (ixesha) |
| 3 | c h m q u y | cat; hat; moon; queen; umbrella; yo-yo | spoon (icephe); pig (ihagu); car (imoto); egg (iqanda); tree (umthi); medicine (iyeza) |
| 4 | d i n r v z | dog; insect; net; ring; van; zebra | duck (idada); dog (inja); nest; traffic light (iroboti); van (ivani); axe |
| 5 | e j | elephant; jacket | bucket (i-emele); jug (ijug) |

EN x is a child holding a chest x-ray; q is a crowned queen. XH x is a clock. **Artwork approval
remains open:** Masi must choose better XH g/n/z pictures with isiXhosa names in place of the
drum/nest/axe placeholders, and supply the clock artwork, before final printing. The designer
must not resolve those phonics choices alone.

**Q10 writing words** (Q9 in 2024.1): four pictures, visibly numbered **1–4**, each with a blank
writing box. The answer words are reference only; do not print them.

| Number | EN picture | XH picture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | jam | sleeping (lala) |
| 2 | hut | drinking (sela) |
| 3 | fin | money (imali) |
| 4 | net | knee (idolo) |

Leave room for the full XH words, including the initial `i` in `imali` and `idolo`.

**Q11 writing sentences** (Q10 in 2024.1): two numbered writing lines, 1 and 2. Do not print the
dictation text.

**Q12 story writing** (Q11 in 2024.1): ruled story-writing lines and a small strip headed **Head
Office use only**, with fields **Meaning Making / Spelling / Length / Vocab / Total /16**. This is
the only paper marking strip; it is for Head Office calibration.

**Footer:** the exact Writing Sheet string for the language, on every page. **Quantity:** one
consumable sheet per child per WelaPLUS assessment, plus 10%. A child whose first row of
letters (Q1), letter writing (Q5) or words (Q6) is all wrong skips Q10–Q12, so part of the
sheet stays blank; that is expected.

The app asks for **one** acknowledgment before finalizing a WelaPLUS assessment:
`Write assessment code <code> and <first name>'s name on the Writing Sheet`. It needs no printed
checkbox.

## Proof approval and source record

Before producing stock, Masi and the designer approve the complete physical pack for each
language: exact text and footers, case-sensitive letter charts, first rows, word-row order,
story paragraphs, side D sentences, Q5 artwork, numbered Q10 order, handwriting space and the
assessment-code field. First-language reviewers check the isiXhosa, Afrikaans and isiZulu proofs
against the source charts, and the pack's open `sourceNotes` are resolved before field use.
Retain the approved editable masters with the versioned PDFs. Any content or order change goes
back to Masi's assessment owner so paper and app change version together.
