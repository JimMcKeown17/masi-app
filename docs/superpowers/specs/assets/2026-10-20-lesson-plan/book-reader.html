What should a book look like inside the app?

Real example: Molo, Lolo! (step 1, the first draft book). Page 3 of 5: Malo says "Lolo! Molo!". Each child voices one character in pair reading, so the colour of the speech bubble tells them whose line it is.

A

Text pages: big type, speech bubbles in each character's colour

Works the day the words are approved, and on every phone. Tiny (a whole library is a few hundred KB), so it ships with ordinary app updates and works offline. No pictures.

B

Picture pages: the printed page itself, art and all

Same as holding the book. Needs the art finished and approved first. Each book is a few MB, so pages download once over Wi-Fi and are then kept offline, rather than shipping inside the app.

C

A now, B later: text pages first, pictures switch on per book once its art is approved

Testing isn't held up by the art. The same reader screen shows pictures for a book when its pages have been downloaded, and text otherwise.
