One way to mark every question

Every question except the story-writing rubric, in WelaPLUS and Full EGRA. Each screen keeps today's layout. Question 1 (letter sounds) already works this way.

The rule an EA learns once: a black ring shows the item to mark → tap Correct or Incorrect → the ring moves on. Made a mistake? Tap Back, or tap any marked item to flip it. Ended early? Anything unmarked is left out of the score.
