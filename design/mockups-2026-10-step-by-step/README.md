# Step-by-step marking for every Question (mockups, 2026-10-25)

`step-by-step.html` holds eight phone frames at 360 dp. Jim approved them on 2026-10-25 ("yes, that all
looks correct"). Open the file in a browser. The story text, sentences and passage are placeholders;
real content comes from the item sets.

| Frame | Shows |
|---|---|
| 1 | Instructions: every Question ends with the same marking sentence |
| 2 | Story questions: today's cards, with a ring on the question being asked |
| 3 | Tap-to-flip: a marked card flips, the ring stays, and a short message confirms it |
| 4 | Writing words (Q9): today's layout, stepping letter by letter |
| 5 | Writing sentences (Q10): word by word inside each sentence |
| 6 | Passage reading (Q8 and EGRA): word by word with the timer, and no last-word step |
| 7 | Review after the last item: no ring, Finish, and taps still flip |
| 8 | Ending early: "The N not marked won't count towards the score" |
