How a phone page maps to the printed card

The child reads the card. The EA marks the phone. Each phone page shows whole card rows, re-flowed into bigger tiles, and its label names the card rows on screen.

Each card row of 10 becomes two phone rows of 5. Card rows never share a phone row, and a thin divider marks where card row 2 starts. Grid A is 3 pages; the future isiZulu card (11 rows) would be 6.

Words read left to right, two per phone row, so a card row of 5 doesn't split evenly: "indlu" (end of card row 1) sits beside "isikolo" (start of row 2). This is how Zazi did it. Each page still holds exactly two whole card rows.
