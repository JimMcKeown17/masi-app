You have six candidate destinations (Home, My Children, Groups, Sessions, Assess, Insights) plus Settings. Something has to give, and the group-centric rebuild will only make that tighter.

N3 · Same shape, but the centre button does something
Home · Children · [ + Record ] · Groups · Assess

Identical geometry to your sketch: one big centre target, two tabs each side. The only change is what it means. It is not a destination, it is the action: record a session, from anywhere in the app, in one tap.

An EA records 3 to 5 sessions a day, every working day. They look at Insights maybe weekly. Optimise the path that is walked 20x more often. Insights moves to a chart icon in the Home header, next to the gear, which is still a big improvement on cluttering the home page.
